⚙ THE AI DIRECTOR

Run any enemy without a brain. The figure's Temperament tells it what to do; you just read the first line that fits. CUS v0.6 · PvE/PvAI.

Three layers — Mission, Order, Figure

1 · The MISSION — what the whole force wants (set once per fight)
Gives every enemy its Focus — where it heads when it isn't already fighting.
OVERRUN — reach the players' edge DEFEND — hold a place DEVOUR — kill everything RAID — grab it & flee HUNT — converge on the weakest hero
2 · The ORDER — the ploy this turn (the smart bit) · ~20 sec, command GROUPS not models
3 · The FIGURE — each enemy's footwork (Temperament). Read the first rule that fits.

2 · The Commander — three moves a turn

A
Pick the seam (the Schwerpunkt). One weakest point to break: a wounded hero, an exposed figure (no friends near), the thinnest squad, a shield-brother whose intercept is spent. Everything aims here.
B
Tag your groups by what they are:
SCREEN · chaff → thrown in first to eat Counters & burn reactions FIX · line/anchors → pin the front so it can't reposition SHAPE · ranged/casters → gall & knock the seam before the hammer FLANK · fast/mounted/flyers → swing to its flank & rear HAMMER · brutes/elites/monster → commit THROUGH the seam
C
Activate in this order: SCREEN → FIX → SHAPE → FLANK → HAMMER. Throwaways first to bait their reactions; the hammer last, after they've shown their hand. That sequence alone is why it reads as cunning.

This is the exact logic the sim's Commander runs — and in testing it beats the dumb AI 52–87% while games stay 5–9 rounds. Fast, mean, coherent.

3 · Activate one enemy → the first thing that fits

0
Broken? → it Routs by Temperament (table below) and stops. · Prone? spend 1 AP to stand.
B
Boss / leader? → fire its special first — Raise a minion · Curse/Dread the party · Command (rally its own) — then act below.
R
Has a ranged weapon & a target in range + line of sight? → if it's Cowardly or Resolute, shoot (Cowardly then steps back). Aggressive/Ravenous prefer to close.
T
Otherwise act by Temperament ↓
AGGRESSIVE
Charge the nearest enemy it can reach this activation. Can't reach one? Advance at the nearest.
RAVENOUS
Charge the nearest figure of ANY side it can reach. Else advance at the nearest. (All Mindless undead use this.)
RESOLUTE
On/next to its Focus? Hold and strike anything adjacent. Not there yet? Advance to the Focus, engaging what's in the way.
COWARDLY
Only pile in with the odds (2+ friends already on that target). Otherwise keep your distance — shoot, and edge toward the Focus staying at range.
PROTECTIVE
Move to the nearest wounded ally or the leader; interpose / heal. Strike only what's already adjacent.
MINDLESS
Undead override — ignore all the above:
Shamble the full move at the nearest LIVING thing and hit it. Never retreat, never test Nerve, never shoot unless it's the only option.

Who does it hit? (target priority)

When a figure can reach more than one, it strikes the one it can most likely finish:
  1. Most-wounded target in reach
  2. else the nearest
  3. else the one whose flank or rear it can reach (no Counter, +dice)

Build the encounter (slot budget)

Total the party's slots — count a Champion as ~4 and each squad as ~6 (a Sergeant + its retinue). Then field enemies to:
×1 party slots — easy / warm-up ×1.5 — a fair fight ×2 — hard, bring your A-game
Each enemy's SLOT is on its card (chaff ½ · trooper 1 · elite 2 · brute 3–4). A Boss's slot value is the encounter — a slot-8 Lich ≈ a whole ~8-slot party fight on its own. Pad with chaff (Zombies, Skeletons) to fill.
