1. **CON-CAND-001 (Nerve).** The deepest structural split. B (2026-07-28) and C (the Dictionary) rebuilt Nerve into a tiered *save* rolled per incoming Morale point and explicitly deleted the 3-dice test — yet **D presents the 3-dice test as SIGNED**, and **H's entire aftermath system (Care/Nerve checks) is built on the "same three-dice, count-successes shape (B·10)"** — citing a B·10 that no longer contains it.
