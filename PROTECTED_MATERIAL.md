## The SOUL tripwire — quoted, not paraphrased

The First Corpus is explicit and self-guarding on SOUL. Its signed ruling
(`E_OPEN_DECISIONS.md:341`) instructs that SOUL is *a formal, undefined Kernel primitive,
permanently blank — never named, called, triggered, read, or state-changed*, and carries a
**tripwire**: the blank is *intentional and signed — never "fix" it.*

Migration consequence:
- No agent, pass, or Second Corpus module may assign SOUL a value, a scale, a trigger, or a read.
- A "helpful" definition of SOUL is a **charter violation**, not an improvement.
- SOUL may be *witnessed* only where Body and Mind interact — it is observed through its
  manifestations, never closed into a variable.

## Redaction as custody (not a First-Corpus mechanism)

The First Corpus does not contain a named redaction mechanism. Redaction is a **Concordance
discipline**: when future material must be concealed from public canon, it is marked `REDACTED`,
concealed in the Second Corpus's public face, and preserved in full in custody — with a path left
for a determined reader. The custodial test:

> Does the redaction preserve an encounter, or merely manufacture vagueness?

If a candidate for redaction appears, it is recorded here before anything is concealed.
