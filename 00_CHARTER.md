# 00 — Archive Charter

The charter that governs the relationship between the frozen First Corpus and everything built
after it. Binding on all migration work.

## The First Corpus

1. The First Corpus (`cus-kernel-rebuild`, tag `first-corpus-v0.6`, commit `ea79b70`) is
   **historical evidence**.
2. It may contain contradictions, abandoned ideas, rhetorical excess, mythological language,
   experiments, and unresolved questions.
3. **These are not defects to be retroactively repaired.**
4. No file in the frozen corpus may be edited. The repository is archived (read-only) to enforce
   this at the platform level.
5. Later commentary, correction, and interpretation are stored here, as annotations — never by
   altering the source.
6. The Second Corpus does not replace the *historical existence* of the First. It succeeds it; it
   does not erase it.

## Corrections

A "correction" to the First Corpus is never an edit to the First Corpus. It is a Concordance
entry (`CON-####`) that:
- cites the exact First Corpus source,
- states what is now understood differently,
- and, where applicable, points to the Second Corpus destination.

## Protected material

Certain material is protected and non-reducible — see [PROTECTED_MATERIAL.md](PROTECTED_MATERIAL.md).
It is preserved in full in the First Corpus. No later rationalization pass may reclassify it as
decorative debris or collapse it into a quantified subsystem.

## The governing rule

> **Nothing migrates merely because it existed, and nothing is removed merely because it cannot
> yet be measured.**
