THE FIELD GUIDE

Two pages. Everything needed to play a first game of CUS, in the order it comes up at the table. Hand it to whoever is learning and let them read it as they go.

  • Print at 100% scale, single-sided, then staple the corner.
  • Page 1 is the activation. Page 2 is the blow and what follows.
  • Pure black and white — no colour needed.
