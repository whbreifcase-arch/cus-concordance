THE EVENT DECK

36 rule cards, one per thing that can happen on the table. Poker size, nine to a sheet. Hand them to whoever is learning; when something happens they do not understand, they find the card by what just happened, not by rule name.

  • Print at 100% scale — not "fit to page", or the cards come out undersized.
  • Cards share cut lines: one cut serves two cards.
  • Sheets 1–4 are the deck. Sheet 5 is the key card plus blanks for your own notes.
  • Pure black and white — no colour needed.
