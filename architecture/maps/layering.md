## PART 2 — LAYER VIOLATIONS

**V1 — Persistence is placed as a stratum but is declared architecture.** [S: Ruling 5]
The stack gives Persistence its own layer (L7), above modules. But the binding ruling says Persistence is *kernel architecture, not a module*. Architecture spans the whole stack; it cannot be a single rung. Compounding this, the persistence **content** (Injury/Scar/Bond/Caravan/TEND) is genuinely durable **runtime state** — i.e. L2 that survives between events — while the persistence **contract** (harm lifecycle, Sealed Resolver, the loop) is architectural. L7 conflates the two.

**V2 — Figure is depended on by layers below its placement.** [SI]
If the Figure→…→World hierarchy is filed at L8, then L2–L5 leak *upward*: the Figure "owns Role·Tool·PACKET refs·stats" (L1 data) and is the actor that MOVE/ACTION/WAIT (L3) consume AP from. A resolution procedure (L5) cannot depend on an institution layer (L8) for the very agent it resolves. **Figure, and the base-agent facets of Square/Circle/RANK/CONTROL, are L1/L2 primitives.** Only the *formations* (Fireteam/Banner/Army/World/Mission) are L8.

**V3 — The governance/jurisprudence pillar has no layer.** [S: "Four pillars… jurisprudence"]
Laws, the Digest, Verdict, Remedy ladder, Decision Register, rationale register, Migration Map, Sim-policy are cited *pervasively* and shape every other layer, yet none of L0–L11 can hold them. They form a governance **column**, orthogonal to the runtime stack. A stack that omits them mislocates its own control plane. Note the Remedy ladder is explicitly *cross-layer* ("each rung is a different layer's tool") — it cannot itself be *in* a layer.

**V4 — L11 duplicates lower layers and re-claims a reserved primitive.** [SI]
Every candidate L11 resident (hollow, Attrition, Bonds-as-meaning, Standing-as-belief) is an interpretive reading of an L2/L7 state, not an independent concept. Its only true anchor, SOUL, is reserved to L0. L11 therefore holds no concept it owns outright.

**V5 — "The base is the instrument" collapses L9 into L1/L2.** [S]
`size_class`, `mounted`, engagement, Reach, and Form Up all read the physical base footprint. The base is simultaneously the **Presentation component** (L9) and the **canonical data source** (L1/L2 Definition). This is a deliberate collapse ("the base does not lie"), but it means L5 procedures read geometry off an L9 object — a real cross-layer coupling the stack should name, not hide.

**V6 — The Decision loop (A·XII) is cross-cutting, not an L5 procedure.** [S: "the same loop at every scale… run at campaign scale it IS the campaign loop; at design scale it drives the Digest."]
Like Persistence, it spans L0–L11 and even reaches the governance column (the Digest). Filing it at any single rung understates it.

**V7 — STUN (L4/L5) rides the L9 activation flag.** [S: "rides the existing flag, no new marker"]
A resolution Effect is constrained by a Presentation-layer component decision (Component Law: no new marker). The *state* Stun writes is L2 (downward, fine), but the "no new marker" constraint is an L9 rule shaping an L4 Effect — a mild upward coupling, acceptable only because Law 12 forbids the component from *forking* the mechanic.

**V8 — Behaviour (L5) tests for Mission (L8) presence.** [SI]
Temperament "drives leaderless behaviour" — i.e. procedures branch on whether an L8 Mission exists. Higher-layer *configuration* of lower layers is legitimate (Mission "narrows, never puppeteers"), but a leaderless-fallback check is L5 reading an L8 object's absence. Minor; treat Mission as input data, not control flow.

### Contradictions the binding rulings do NOT cover — flagged, not resolved
- **E-LAW-136:** the aftermath 3-dice count-successes "Nerve/Care check" (Persistence, L7) collides with **Nerve-as-a-save** (L4, Ruling 1). L7 carries a *second, incompatible* resolution model for the same word. Cross-layer duplication — unreconciled.
- **C-LAW-C01/C02:** **Push** (charge plow, L5 Position-write) vs **Shove** (weapon Effect); and the **Charge** term across Resource (L1/L2), qualified Sprint (L5), and a retired keyword. Ruling 4 fixes the Resource name only; the Push/Shove split is *not* ruling-covered.
- **I-LAW-070:** Moved-eligibility of an NPC Circle — the **RANK** axis ("Squares only") vs the **CONTROL** axis ("an NPC Circle CAN be Moved"). Two L1 axes disagree; unresolved.
- **K-LAW-047:** "a shot never draws a Counter" vs **Overwatch** as an out-of-turn reaction (L5). Unresolved.
- **M vs its stat blocks:** Doc M "invents no rule" (L10 content) yet prints numbers that look like L4/L2 values (e.g. Orks: 3 Wounds, no Shrug). Potential L10→L4 leak; unresolved.
- **SOUL:** recorded RESERVED only. No definition/value/trigger/mechanic exists or is proposed. Honored.

## PART 3 — VERDICT

**Does the stack hold? Mostly — L0–L5, L9, L10 are sound; L7, L8, L11, and the missing governance column are not.**

The strong half is not a coincidence: L1/L2/L3-L4/L9 are essentially CUS's own four-layer model — **Definition · Instance · Procedure · Presentation** (A·IX) — with the candidate stack splitting "Procedure" into intent-grammar (L3), resolution-grammar (L4), and owned procedures (L5). That split is directly supported by **Law 6 (intent vs resolution)**, **Invocation** (three player verbs / five engine invocations), and **Law 14 (one owned Procedure per mechanic)**. Where the candidate stack refines CUS's own layering, it holds. [S]

The weak half comes from three category errors:

1. **Two "layers" are actually cross-cutting architecture, not strata.** Persistence (Ruling 5) and the Decision loop (A·XII) both span every scale. **Change: reorder them out of the linear stack.** Split Persistence into (a) durable state → **merge into L2** as an explicitly "surviving" sub-band (Injury/Scar/Bond/Caravan/Composure-carry), and (b) the harm-lifecycle contract + Sealed Resolver → an **architecture band adjacent to L1**. Do the same for the Decision loop.

2. **The entity hierarchy is filed at one altitude but spans three.** **Change: split L8.** The Figure (and the base-agent facets of Square/Circle/RANK/CONTROL) drop to **L1/L2**; only Fireteam/Banner/Army/World/Mission/Doctrine/Scenario/Horde stay at L8. This removes the upward dependency in V2.

3. **The stack has no control plane.** **Change: add an orthogonal governance column** (not a rung): Laws, Digest, Verdict, Remedy ladder, Decision Register, rationale/Migration registers, Sim-policy. The Remedy ladder is itself proof this must be orthogonal — its four rungs each act on a *different* layer.

**Merges / demotions:**
- **Merge L11 into an interpretive annotation over L7/L2**, or fold into L10. It owns no concept outright; its only true anchor (SOUL) is L0-reserved and must stay inert. Keep the *name* L11 only if you want a labelled reading-frame — no mechanics may ever land there.
- **Merge durable persistence-state into L2** (per change 1).
- **Keep L3/L4 split** — it is the stack's best-supported seam.

**Reorder:** entity primitive (Figure) must precede the procedures that consume it; today's L8 placement inverts that. Corrected order of the runtime spine: L0 → L1 (incl. Figure) → L2 (incl. durable state) → L3 → L4 → L5 → L6 (modules) → L8 (institutions/campaign) → L9 → L10, with L7-architecture and the Decision loop pulled alongside L1 as cross-cutting, L11 demoted to annotation, and governance sitting as an orthogonal column.

**Net:** the candidate stack is a good *refinement* of CUS's native Definition/Instance/Procedure/Presentation model for L0–L5/L9/L10, but it mistakes two architectural spanners (Persistence, the loop) for layers, misplaces the Figure, has no home for the jurisprudence pillar, and carries a redundant mythic layer. Fix those four and it holds. The five ruling-uncovered contradictions (E-LAW-136 chief among them, since it puts a live second resolution model inside the persistence band) are pre-existing and remain **open** — they are not layering artifacts and are not resolved here.
