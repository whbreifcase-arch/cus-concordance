# PRIMITIVE-REDUCTION ANALYSIS — CUS Kernel

Method note: the corpus has *already performed* most available reductions (Pin→Stun; Suppress→Morale channel; Reaction pool→Position/`provokes`/once-per-occurrence; Ladder/Tiers/Rungs→Grade; five Roles→three; alignment-tag REJECTED; ATTACK/USE→ACTION, READY→WAIT, Advance→MOVE). That track record is evidence the designers squeeze where squeezing is safe. What remains is what *resisted*. My job is to show where residual pressure sits and price each merge — not to re-open closed rulings.

---

## (1) MINIMAL PRIMITIVE SET I CAN DEFEND

I cannot get below the corpus's own floor. The set below is irreducible; each line is a necessity proof.

**Substrates (touched by every read/write):**
- **POSITION** — SOURCE (Position; Ruling 8; front-arc). *Necessity:* sole unsaveable relational geometry; it is the cap on out-of-turn response (front arc, after the Reaction pool was struck) AND the only social armour ("hierarchy is armour"). Standing is *defined as* a Position fact (Ruling 8). No carrier expresses "where, relative to." 
- **FORCE** — SOURCE (Four axes; Force SIGNED 2026-07-24). *Necessity:* the single referent all four axes classify — Pressure *applies*, Anchor *prevents*, Utility *changes* Force. Delete it and the axes lose their common object. Non-numerical, so uncollapsible into any carrier.

**Carriers (how a substrate is stored/spent):**
- **STATE** — SOURCE (Ruling 7; Channel of harm). *Necessity:* sole home of saveable, terminal truth (Body/Mind three-state tracks; Wounds/Composure as numbers). Morale was migrated *into* State (Ruling 7) — the boundary is actively policed. 
- **RESOURCE** — SOURCE (Resource kind A·IV; Agency/Charge/Strain). *Necessity:* the only spend-and-replenish quantity, typed by replenishment (🟢renewing/🟡finite/🔴accumulating). AP, Charge, Strain, Time need "spend" semantics State does not carry.

**Reserved:**
- **SOUL** — RESERVED / PROTECTED / **NON-REDUCIBLE**. Listed only. No definition, value, trigger, or mechanic proposed. Structural twin of Force (the other blank non-numerical primitive) — but unlike Force it is never read.

**Verbs (player vocabulary):**
- **MOVE** — *Necessity:* the only verb that writes Position **without a PACKET/Grade**. 
- **ACTION** — *Necessity:* the only verb that resolves a PACKET into Effects via a Grade. 
- **WAIT** — *Necessity:* the only AP-priced verb that arms a PACKET against a *future* trigger window ("arming is permission," 2026-07-27).

**Grammar object:**
- **PACKET** — *Necessity:* one neutral executable object referenced by all five invocations; already maximally reduced (no good/bad tag — alignment REJECTED, signed E). Removing it re-forks a type per invocation.

**Reduction the corpus already implies (I ratify it):** the **four axes are NOT primitives** — they are classifications *of* Force (Doctrine is explicitly "not a fifth axis"). The reduction runs axes→Force, never Force→axes.

## (2) CANDIDATE MERGES / DUPLICATES — with LOSS REPORTS

### MERGE-A: Resource → State (collapse the two carriers)
STRUCTURAL INFERENCE. Argument: both are "carriers"; Morale already crossed from Resource to State (Ruling 7); Wounds/Composure are "a number, not a track" held in Instance — indistinguishable in storage from a pool.
- **LOSS REPORT:** you lose the replenishment typing (🟢/🟡/🔴) that "scopes costs." State has no native *spend* or *replenish* verb, no threshold-that-vents. The entire firearms economy — Reload = card-down, Overcharge = spend-limiter, Strain "pops at 3," Overheat→VENT/BLOW-UP — is unstatable.
- **RESISTS (historical):** Ruling 7 *removed* Morale from Resource — a deliberate boundary-draw only meaningful if the two categories are distinct. The colour+shape taxonomy (A·IV) is maintained. The Reaction pool was reasoned about as "is/isn't a Resource" before being struck.
- **EMERGENT LOST:** staggered reloads ("synchronized-empty reload is the designed catastrophe"); Strain brinkmanship; "the limiter is not ammo — it is attrition" (framed *against* Resource depletion).
- **VERDICT: KEEP distinct.** Boundary sharpens rather than merges: damage-on-a-track = State; spent-to-act = Resource.

### MERGE-B: Position → State
STRUCTURAL INFERENCE. Argument: Position is written (by MOVE/Impact); facing/engagement/footprint are "what is currently true."
- **LOSS REPORT:** Position is a *substrate* (changed, not spent; read by nearly every procedure) and is **unsaveable** — nothing rolls a save against geometry. Ruling 8 fixes Standing as "a Position fact, **not a meter**"; collapsing to State re-meterizes Standing, which Ruling 8 forbids.
- **RESISTS:** Ruling 8; the Reaction-pool cap was redistributed *to Position* (front arc), not to State; "the base is the instrument" (geometry read, not stored truth).
- **EMERGENT LOST:** "a screen beats a charge," flanking, the Push plow (Push→Indent→Crush), social "hierarchy made of other people."
- **VERDICT: KEEP distinct.**

### MERGE-C: Force → axes (eliminate Force, keep the four axes as primitives)
STRUCTURAL INFERENCE. Argument: Force has "no stat, no roll," is only "read via the four axes" — perhaps it is just their union.
- **LOSS REPORT:** the axes are *defined in terms of* Force (apply/prevent/change/deliver Force); orphan them and they float without a common object. You also sever the signed Force↔SOUL twin-blank link that structurally supports the SOUL tripwire.
- **RESISTS:** Force SIGNED 2026-07-24 as a primitive; the Force–SOUL cross-link is deliberate.
- **EMERGENT LOST:** Temperament/Rout read "several legal applications of Force" — the bias needs a referent to bias among; Archetype (Role+Tool+signature) presupposes Force.
- **VERDICT: KEEP Force; DEMOTE axes** (they were never primitives).

### MERGE-D: WAIT → ACTION (WAIT as a deferred ACTION)
STRUCTURAL INFERENCE. Argument: both spend Agency, both operate on PACKETs.
- **LOSS REPORT:** you lose the only AP-priced out-of-turn capability and the intent-vs-resolution split (Law 6). WAIT *arms* (buys a trigger window, expires next activation); ACTION *resolves now* — distinct temporal semantics. The facedown card ("the WAIT trap," Component Layer) is a physical affordance requiring WAIT's own identity.
- **RESISTS:** the 3-verbs/5-invocations grammar keeps WAIT separate from WRITTEN_TRIGGER precisely because WAIT is *AP-priced and player-armed* while triggers fire free; Story mirrors it as Prepare/Vow.
- **EMERGENT LOST:** Overwatch shot-ladders, Brace facing-trades, armed defensive lines.
- **VERDICT: KEEP distinct.**

### MERGE-E: MOVE → ACTION (MOVE as an ACTION with a self-targeted Position-write Effect) — *thinnest boundary*
STRUCTURAL INFERENCE. This is the strongest merge because the Effect layer **already writes Position** (Shove; the Push). So "only MOVE writes Position" is *false* — the clean claim is "only MOVE writes Position **without a PACKET/Grade**."
- **LOSS REPORT:** merging forces every plain reposition through a null-PACKET + trivial Grade + target-legality, re-introducing the exact Grade machinery MOVE avoids and converting a self-act ("acts on the actor") into a target-resolved act. Impact (an OWNED_PROCEDURE that a MOVE *creates*) would become an Effect of itself — circular.
- **RESISTS:** Advance→MOVE and ATTACK/USE→ACTION were folded as *two* verbs, deliberately kept apart (SIGNED 2026-07-25, "MOVE keeps its name in every module").
- **EMERGENT LOST:** the qualified-Sprint "surge"/charge is emergent from MOVE under the activation economy (Doc F: clash is "an emergent state," ½–1″ increments); merging removes incremental movement, so "a screen beats a charge" and "contact commits the charge where it happens" both die; the Push (Position-only, no Grade) would need a Grade.
- **VERDICT: KEEP distinct — flagged as the leakiest boundary.** Held by three independent separators: roll-vs-no-roll, self-vs-target, Position-direct-vs-State-via-Grade.

### MERGE-F: 3 verbs → 5 invocations (drop the player/engine split)
STRUCTURAL INFERENCE. Argument: why keep both "three verbs" and "five invocations"?
- **LOSS REPORT:** the split is a **player/engine membrane** (A·XIV). WRITTEN_TRIGGER and OWNED_PROCEDURE are engine-only and *free*; handing them to players as choosable verbs makes Counter, Retort, and Shield Intercept spendable and breaks "the floor is never zero" (Fists always Counter).
- **RESISTS:** the Reaction-pool retirement moved authoring to `provokes` and anti-milking to once-per-occurrence *specifically to keep triggers engine-side and free*.
- **VERDICT: KEEP the split.** Not a duplication.

### DUPLICATE FLAGS (name-level, not primitive-level)
- **Push (plow) vs Shove (Effect):** STRUCTURAL INFERENCE — these are the *same primitive operation* (Position-displacement) reached by two invocations (OWNED_PROCEDURE Impact vs ACTION Grade-Effect). The split is a NAME problem (C-LAW-C01), **NOT covered by any binding ruling** — flagged, not resolved.
- **Nerve-as-save (Ruling 1) vs aftermath 3-dice "Nerve/Care check" (E-LAW-136):** one *name* over two different resolution structures (tiered save vs count-successes roll). **Live contradiction the rulings do not reconcile** — flagged, not resolved.
- **Charge (Resource) vs charge/"surge" (movement):** already separated by Ruling 4 — respected, not merged.

## (3) IRREDUCIBLE DISTINCTIONS

1. **Substrate ⟂ Carrier.** {Position, Force} are *touched* by reads/writes; {State, Resource} *store/spend*. No merge crosses this line.
2. **Position ⟂ Force** (within substrates): unsaveable relational geometry vs non-numerical capacity/axis-referent. No shared reduction.
3. **State ⟂ Resource** (within carriers): "what is true" (saveable, terminal) vs "what remains to spend" (replenishment-typed). Morale's migration (Ruling 7) *proves* the boundary is policed, not collapsible.
4. **MOVE ⟂ ACTION ⟂ WAIT:** Position-write-without-Grade vs PACKET-resolution-via-Grade vs arm-against-future-trigger. Separable on substrate, roll, and temporality. (MOVE/ACTION is the thinnest — held only because Effects that write Position always carry a Grade and a target, which MOVE never does.)
5. **Player-verbs (3) ⟂ engine-invocations (5):** a sovereignty membrane, not redundancy. WRITTEN_TRIGGER / OWNED_PROCEDURE must stay engine-side and free.
6. **Force ⟂ SOUL:** both formal, non-numerical, blank — but Force is *read* (via axes) and SOUL is *never read*. SOUL is **PROTECTED / NON-REDUCIBLE**; not operationalized here.

**Bottom line:** the defensible minimum equals the corpus's stated set — four substrate/carrier primitives + reserved SOUL, three verbs, one PACKET grammar — with the axes correctly demoted to classifications of Force. The system already sits at its irreducible floor. Residual pressure is concentrated at exactly three seams (Resource/State carrier line, MOVE/ACTION verb line, and the Push/Shove + Nerve-name duplications), and two of those duplications sit in the gap the binding rulings leave open.
