# Wave 2 Rulings — CON-0011 … CON-0013

Signed decisions on the three structural risks Architecture Zero surfaced that needed a call.
Ruled by William, 2026-07-28. Concordance rulings — they direct the Second Corpus rebuild; the
frozen First Corpus is untouched.

---

## CON-0011 — The dual "Nerve" (Architecture Zero risk #1)
**Disposition:** RESOLVED.
- **Problem:** "Nerve" named two incompatible resolvers — the combat tiered **save** (Ruling 1) and the aftermath **3-dice check** (H/E, E-LAW-136).
- **Ruling:** **"Nerve" means only the combat tiered save** (psychic armour on the Mind channel). The aftermath 3-dice check is **renamed the "Morale check"** — a recovery test on the Mind channel.
- **The Mind channel family is now clean:** damage = **Morale**, save = **Nerve**, aftermath recovery roll = **Morale check**. No word names two resolvers.
- **Destination:** Second Corpus / Combat (Nerve = save) + Persistence spanner (Morale check = the aftermath roll).

## CON-0012 — Push vs Shove (risk #4)
**Disposition:** RESOLVED · **keep Push.**
- **Ruling:** Displacement that writes Position is **Push** — the one canonical name. A weapon that displaces produces a **Push as a PACKET Effect** (same primitive, delivered by a different invocation). **"Shove" is retired** as a separate mechanic-name.
- **Note:** the continuous charge-plow Push and a weapon's knockback Push are the *same* primitive (write Position); if a weapon needs a bigger/different displacement it is a **named field on the Effect**, not a new verb (Law 15).
- **Destination:** Second Corpus / Combat (Push) + Dictionary (strike "Shove" as a distinct term).

## CON-0013 — Circles in formation movement (risk #6, physical side)
**Disposition:** RESOLVED.
- **Ruling:** **Circles do not move in formation.** Formation movement (Form Up / group MOVE) carries **Squares** (rank-and-file) only. A **Circle** (hero / champion / leader) moves solely on its own Agency — it is never swept along by a formation.
- **Note:** this settles the *physical* Rank/Control eligibility question. The Story sense of "Moved" (persuasion → Bond, I-LAW-070) is a separate axis and is **left as-is** for a later Story pass.
- **Destination:** Second Corpus / Combat (formation movement excludes Circles).
