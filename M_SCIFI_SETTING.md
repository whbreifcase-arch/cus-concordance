# CUS — THE SCI-FI SETTING
### v0.6 · a content pack, not the Kernel · signed 2026-07-28 (William)

> **What this is.** A **setting** — the far-future war of golden-child elites, endless
> hordes, and hard-tech guns. It is **content**, not architecture: it declares four
> factions built entirely from the Kernel (A), the Combat Module (B), the Firearm
> engine ([K](K_FIREARMS.md)), and the horde/campaign spine ([L](L_HORDE_AND_CAMPAIGN.md)).
> It **invents no rule.** Every faction is Role · Tempo · Tool · Temperament · base ·
> stats · traits · packets, the way the fantasy warbands are.
>
> **One engine, two settings.** CUS's fantasy world fields **blackpowder** (K·11); this
> setting fields **autoguns, plasma, and heavy weapons.** Same engine, different
> content — you never mix an autogun into a fantasy warband, and you never need to.
> `→ G·one-engine-any-era`
>
> **Authority.** M is the lowest layer — pure content. Where it conflicts with A/B/K/L,
> they win. Numbers here are **archetypes, not tuned prices**; the pricer
> (`factions/sim`) sets the points.
>
> **Resource skins.** Weapons here author against the kernel Resources (A·IV): ballistic
> guns **spend Charge** and energy weapons **gain Strain**. This setting *skins* them —
> Charge shows as **ammo / magazines**, Strain as **reactor heat**. Where the text below
> says "ammo" or "heat," it means those skins over Charge/Strain, never a separate system.

# The through-line — four answers to the horde

Each faction is a distinct **relationship with the gun system** and a distinct answer to
the survival question (L·5): *how do you not die to the wave?*

| Faction | Identity | Survives the horde by… |
|---|---|---|
| **Space Marines** | Golden-child elite — few, versatile, unbreakable | Out-classing it: spray + armour + no fear |
| **Astra Militarum** | Combined arms — weak alone, lethal as a system | Numbers + **artillery** + orders; an attrition race |
| **Tau** | Hard-to-hit sharpshooters | Never being *reached* — kite, deny the crossing |
| **Orks** | Berserkers — they *want* the melee | Out-*aggressing* it: race the nest and chop it |

# Matchup philosophy — asymmetric, balanced by scenario — SIGNED (William, 2026-07-28)

Factions **hard-counter each other**, and that is the point. Fairness comes from the
**scenario** (map · objective · initiative · points-as-budget) and campaign handicaps —
**not** from stat symmetry. Symmetry would forbid hard counters and sand the identities
flat. This is what CUS already is (PvE-first, scenario-authored, multi-area maps
mandatory). Curated "picked" matchups are the **feature**. `→ G·asymmetry-is-not-imbalance`

## The 50/50 mode is a MIRROR — SIGNED
When players want a dead-even skill test, they play the **same faction, different
sub-faction** (Marine Chapters · Ork Clans · Tau Septs · Guard Regiments): identical
chassis = guaranteed symmetry; flavor divergence = variety that **never touches the
chassis.** CUS therefore never needs cross-faction balance. `→ G·the-fair-game-is-a-mirror`

## The counter-web (content symmetry would erase)
```text
TAU     > static gunlines / elites-at-range     < melee that CATCHES them (Orks, Assault)
ORKS    > kiters & gunlines they reach           < artillery/blast (a mob is template food)
                                                    + charge-lane denial (Guard screens/overwatch)
GUARD   > Orks & hordes (blast the blob)          < elite alpha-strike / fast flankers to the guns
MARINES = golden mean; contest all                < a specialist out-doing them on ONE axis
```
Balance levers, in order: **SCENARIO** (map/objective/initiative) → points × budget dial
→ campaign handicap. Unit-stat symmetry is not a lever this game chases.

# 1 · SPACE MARINES — the golden children

- **Chassis:** Heavy armour (4+) · 2 Wounds · Resolute · **Hybrid** (bolter +
  chainsword) · Tempo Normal · Square (leaders Circle). **Few models.**
- **Signature — *They Know No Fear*:** immune to **Pin/Suppress** (K·3); auto-passes the
  first Nerve shock. The wave cannot panic them. Plus **bolter drill** — bigger mags /
  cheaper reload (K·6), so they stagger reloads almost for free.
- **Guns:** ballistic **ammo**; the bolter **sprays** (K·4) and Heavy armour lets them
  tank the wave while mowing it; **overcharge** to punch armour.
- **Roster:** Tactical (bolter Hybrid) · Assault (chainsword + pistol, jump = Fast) ·
  Devastator (heavy weapon — **spray/blast**, ammo-hungry) · Sergeant (Circle, Rally).
- **Horde answer:** the reference *survive* squad — armour shrugs hits, no-fear ignores
  pin, spray clears, overwatch holds the corridor.

# 2 · ASTRA MILITARUM (GUARD) — combined forces

- **Chassis:** Light armour (6+) · 1 Wound · Cowardly · cheap Squares. Fragile alone;
  the **system** is strong.
- **Signature — *Orders*:** a Commander (Circle) issues Orders — an ACTION composing
  **Rally + Form Up + Mission** (B·10/11, A·XI): *"First Rank, Fire!"* = a squad volley;
  *"Bring it Down!"* = concentrated fire for +dice. Weak men made functional by command.
- **Guns:** massed lasgun **ammo** (volley); Guard **own the artillery** — indirect
  **blast + scatter** (K·5) is their signature; Heavy Weapon Teams bring the LMG spray
  (huge ammo burn).
- **Roster:** Guardsman · Heavy Weapon Team (blast/spray) · Artillery (indirect blast) ·
  Commander (Circle, Orders, Rally).
- **Horde answer:** a *disciplined* player-horde — artillery softens the blob, orders
  hold the line, bodies buy time. An attrition race won by positioning.

# 3 · TAU — hard-to-hit sharpshooters

- **Chassis:** Light armour + **Evasion** (K·9) · Fast (7–8" / jetpack) · Cowardly ·
  long precise guns. **Fold in melee** (weak Fists, never want contact).
- **Signature — *Evasion* (an `unpierceable` save, K·9 / B·7):** their save that
  `ignore-Armour` and blast cannot bypass, so you cannot out-gun them, you must **corner**
  them. Plus
  **Fire-and-Fade** (jetpack): shoot, then reposition — but moving makes their own next
  shot Quick-tier (the shot ladder taxes the kiter, so it is a real choice).
- **Guns:** precise (low success number) · long range · **overcharge** to punch Marines ·
  energy weapons **gain Strain** (this setting skins it *reactor heat*) rather than
  spending Charge — they pace fire, never run dry.
- **Roster:** Fire Warrior (pulse, Evasion) · Stealth Suit (Evasion++, infiltrate) ·
  Battlesuit (Large, jetpack, heavy energy, overcharge) · Drone (Small, **Utility**
  spotter — cuts scatter / boosts accuracy).
- **Horde answer:** deny the crossing — kite the wave, never get reached. High skill;
  catastrophic if surrounded.

# 4 · ORKS — berserkers

- **Chassis:** Medium armour (5+) · **3 Wounds (the extra heart) — no special save** ·
  Aggressive/Ravenous · Fast in the charge. They **eat the lane** to reach you and are
  simply *hard to put down.*
- **Signature — *Mob + 'Ere We Go*:** the existing **Mob** dice-pile (more boyz adjacent
  = +dice, B) plus a **berserker Written Trigger** (A·XIV) — wounded → +dice / a free
  swing. They get *better* as it gets worse.
- **Guns:** **dakka** — crude **spray** (K·4) with lots of dice but a *high* success
  number (spray-and-pray), burning ammo insanely fast. The shooting is noise on the way
  in; they would rather charge.
- **Roster:** Boy (choppa + slugga, Mob) · Nob (Circle-ish, tough) · Lootas (dakka
  spray, ammo-hungry) · Warboss (Circle, **Waaagh!** = a faction-wide aggression Mission).
- **Horde answer:** don't defend — *out-aggress*. Race the killing ground, chop the nest
  before the squad decays.

# Sub-factions — the flavor layer (Chapters · Clans · Septs · Regiments)

Each faction carries **sub-factions**: a shared chassis with a doctrine skin — Marine
**Chapters**, Ork **Clans**, Tau **Septs**, Guard **Regiments**. A sub-faction tweaks
**minor traits and doctrine**, **never the chassis**. This is what makes the **mirror
match** fair (same chassis) and still varied (different doctrine), and it is the seam
where campaign identity lives — your Chapter, your grudges, your veterans. It adds **no
rule**: a doctrine is a trait or a Mission bias, both already in the Kernel.

# What this setting owes the Kernel

```text
It OWNS       nothing but CONTENT — four factions and their rosters. No primitive, no
             verb, no Procedure.
It USES       A (Kernel) · B (Combat) · K (Firearms: spray/blast/ammo/heat/overcharge/
             evasion) · L (horde/campaign) · SCENARIO_PROMPT (balance-by-scenario).
It MUST NOT   invent a rule to make a faction work. If a faction seems to need one, the
             rule belongs in B or K and the faction only references it. Every signature
             above is a trait, a Written Trigger, a Mission, or a K mechanic — never new
             machinery.
```

Marines out-class · Guard out-systems · Tau out-ranges · Orks out-aggresses. Four
answers to one wave, all spoken in the one grammar.
